Tips 1-4: Master the Prompt

Tip 1: Use the CRAFT Prompt Formula

TIP 1

CRAFT Formula 2x output quality

The technique: Structure every important US prompt using CRAFT — (C) Context, (R) Role, (A) Action, (F) Format, (T) Tone. This single technique doubles output quality vs basic prompting.

US example: Instead of "write a US LinkedIn post," use: "Context: I'm a US B2B SaaS VP Marketing targeting US CFOs. Role: You are an expert US LinkedIn ghostwriter with 15 years of US B2B experience. Action: Write a 200-word US LinkedIn post about how US companies are using AI to cut US finance costs by 30%. Format: Hook in first line, 3 US bullet points with US stats, US call-to-action at end. Tone: Professional but conversational US tone, no US jargon."

US time saved: 30-50% fewer rewrites per US deliverable. For a US marketer writing 20 pieces per week, that's 4-6 hours saved weekly.

Tip 2: Set Up Custom Instructions

TIP 2

Custom Instructions Auto-applied

The technique: In Settings → Personalization → Custom Instructions, define your US role, US industry, US tone preferences, and US formatting rules. These apply to every US conversation automatically.

US example: "I'm a US product manager at a Seattle US tech company. I write US product specs, US roadmap documents, and US customer emails. Prefer US bullet points, US headers for sections, and US dates in MM/DD/YYYY format. Avoid US filler phrases like 'I hope this email finds you well.' Use direct US tone."

US time saved: 10-15 minutes per US conversation by eliminating repetitive US context-setting. Over a US work year, that's 40+ hours.

Tip 3: Chain Prompts for Complex US Work

TIP 3

Prompt Chaining 5x better output

The technique: For complex US deliverables, chain 4-5 prompts: (1) research the US topic, (2) outline the US structure, (3) draft the US content, (4) critique the US draft, (5) produce the US final. Each builds on the last.

US example: For a US white paper: Prompt 1 = "Research US enterprise AI adoption trends in 2026 with US sources." Prompt 2 = "Create a US outline for a 2,500-word US white paper targeting US CTOs." Prompt 3 = "Draft the US white paper following the US outline." Prompt 4 = "Critique this US draft for US clarity, US evidence strength, and US persuasiveness." Prompt 5 = "Rewrite incorporating the US critique."

US time saved: US deliverables that would take 2 days get done in 3 hours — and the quality is higher than either approach alone.

Tip 4: Use @Mentions to Reference Other Chats

@Mentions Context linking

The technique: In any US conversation, use @ to pull in context from other US chats or US Projects. Type @ and select the US chat you want to reference.

US example: You're drafting a US sales proposal. Type "@Q3 US Strategy" to pull your US Q3 strategy doc into the current US conversation. Then ask: "Based on @Q3 US Strategy, draft a US proposal for [US client] focusing on US AI automation ROI."

US time saved: 5-10 minutes per US task by eliminating re-pasting of US context across conversations.

Tips 5-8: Build Persistent US Context

Tip 5: Turn On Memory

TIP 5

ChatGPT Memory Cross-conversation

The technique: Enable Memory in Settings → Personalization. ChatGPT automatically remembers facts about you — your US job, US preferences, US writing style — across every conversation.

US examples of stored memories: "User is a US product manager in Seattle," "User prefers US bulleted lists," "User always wants US code in TypeScript," "User writes US emails in direct US tone with no pleasantries," "User's US company uses HubSpot CRM."

US time saved: 10-15 minutes per conversation by eliminating repetitive US context. Review and curate memories quarterly to remove outdated US information.

Tip 6: Use Projects for Every US Initiative

TIP 6

ChatGPT Projects Knowledge grounding

The technique: Create a ChatGPT Project for every US client, US project, US department, or US recurring workflow. Upload US documentation as Project Knowledge.

US examples: Project "US Client Acme Corp" with Acme's US brand guide, US product specs, and past US case studies. Project "US Q4 Roadmap" with US PRDs, US customer interviews, and US competitor research. Project "US Hiring" with US role descriptions, US interview rubrics, and US company values.

US time saved: 40% of US context-setting prompts eliminated. The Seattle company built 47 Projects and reported 35% faster US task completion.

TIP 7

Custom GPTs 4x faster tasks

The technique: Identify your 5-10 highest-frequency US workflows and build a dedicated US custom GPT for each. Each GPT encodes your US company's US voice, US standards, and US-specific knowledge.

US examples: "US Sales Email GPT" trained on your US ICP and US brand voice. "US Meeting Summariser GPT" trained on your US meeting format. "US Support Reply GPT" trained on your US knowledge base. "US US Job Description GPT" trained on your US role templates.

US time saved: US workers with 5+ US custom GPTs report 4x faster task completion vs ad-hoc prompting.

Favourite Prompts Quick access

The technique: Bookmark your most-used US prompts in the ChatGPT sidebar. Click the star icon next to any US conversation to favourite it.

US examples: "Weekly US status report," "US customer email reply," "US LinkedIn post draft," "US meeting summary," "US data analysis prompt." Power US users have 20-50 favourite US prompts.

US time saved: US workers with 20+ US favourite prompts complete US tasks 3x faster than those retyping prompts every time.

TIP 15

Critique Loop 80% fewer errors

The technique: After any US draft, ask ChatGPT to critique it for US clarity, US grammar, US tone, and US persuasiveness. Then ask it to US incorporate the critique. This single technique catches 80% of US errors before US publishing.

US example: After drafting a US email: "Critique this US email for: (1) US clarity — is the US ask obvious? (2) US tone — appropriate for a US CFO? (3) US persuasiveness — what would make it stronger? (4) US errors — grammar, typos, US formatting. Then rewrite incorporating the US critique."
