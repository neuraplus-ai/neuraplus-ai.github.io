Gaming Money Missions

The best way to make money in GTA Online depends on how much you already have. With little cash, start with the First Dose missions, the Acid Lab and the Cluckin' Bell Farm Raid, then save toward the Kosatka submarine for the Cayo Perico heist. Weekly event bonuses can change which activity pays best, so check them before a long session. Cheats do not work online (see GTA 5 cheat codes), so these are the real methods.

✅ Short answer: Beginner route: First Dose → Acid Lab → Farm Raid → save $2.2 million for the Kosatka → Cayo Perico on repeat, plus passive businesses like the Nightclub and Bunker.

A beginner-to-pro money route

  1. Start free. Finish the First Dose missions to unlock the Acid Lab. New characters can also use the Career Builder, which gives a few million dollars to get started.
  2. Run the Farm Raid. It is a low-cost earner before you buy big properties.
  3. Buy the Acid Lab upgrade. The $250,000 Equipment Upgrade makes it a strong solo passive business.
  4. Save for the Kosatka. It costs $2.2 million and unlocks the Cayo Perico heist, which can be done solo.
  5. Add passive income. Nightclub and Bunker earn in the background while you play.
  6. Check weekly bonuses. Rockstar's events can make another activity temporarily better.

Solo vs crew play

Solo players should favour the Acid Lab, Bunker, Counterfeit Cash, Payphone Hits, Chop Shop and Agency Contracts, because their sell missions do not demand a team. Crews can make more from heists, but randoms often quit mid-job, so play with friends. Cayo Perico is the main exception because its preparations and finale can be completed solo.

Story mode is different

If you play offline, the money system is completely separate. Read how to make money in GTA 5 story mode for the stock market and heist route, or the older San Andreas cheat codes if you want a money code in a different game.

Money mistakes to avoid

  • Buying cosmetic properties first. Buy the businesses that earn before the ones that only look good.
  • Waiting for full stock. Volume matters, but idle businesses waste time. Sell when it makes sense.
  • Ignoring bonuses. Check the weekly event before choosing what to run.
  • Trusting old payout numbers. Updates change rewards, so treat any figure as a snapshot.
