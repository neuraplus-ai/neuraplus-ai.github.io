This guide shows you exactly how to buy guest posts on US technology sites the right way—what to look for, what to avoid, and how to maximize your ROI.

Why Buy Guest Posts on US Technology Sites?

Buying guest posts on US technology websites offers several strategic advantages:

  • Instant authority: Backlinks from established tech blogs boost your domain authority
  • Targeted traffic: Reach decision-makers actively reading technology content
  • SEO impact: Do-follow backlinks from DA 50+ sites significantly improve rankings
  • Brand credibility: Being published on reputable tech sites builds instant trust
  • Time efficiency: Skip the months-long outreach process and get published faster

When done correctly, buying guest posts is a legitimate, white-hat SEO strategy that delivers real results.

How Much Does It Cost to Buy Guest Posts?

Here's what you can realistically expect to pay for guest posts on US technology sites in 2026:

  • Basic posts (DA 30-50): $200-300 per post
  • Premium posts (DA 50-70): $300-500 per post
  • High-authority posts (DA 70+): $500-1,000+ per post
  • Package deals (10 posts): $2,000-4,000 (10-20% discount)

What affects pricing?

  • Domain Authority (DA) of the publishing site
  • Traffic volume and quality
  • Content length and complexity
  • Number of backlinks included
  • Niche specificity (AI, SaaS, cybersecurity, etc.)

⚠️ Warning: If someone offers you guest posts for under $150 per post on "high-authority tech sites," run. They're either using PBNs (Private Blog Networks), spammy sites, or simply don't know what they're doing. Quality guest posting on legitimate US technology blogs requires real editorial standards and relationships.

Where to Buy Quality Guest Posts on Technology Websites

You have three main options for buying guest posts:

1. Reputable SEO Agencies

Professional agencies have established relationships with technology blog editors and can secure placements on high-authority sites. They handle everything from content creation to publication.

2. Guest Post Marketplaces

Platforms that connect buyers with website owners. Look for marketplaces that vet sites for quality and provide transparent metrics.

3. Direct Outreach

Contacting technology blog editors directly. This takes more time but can result in better pricing and relationships.

For specialized niches, check out resources like our guide on AI niche guest post sites to find relevant opportunities in your sector.

What to Look for When Buying Guest Posts

Not all guest post opportunities are created equal. Here's your checklist:

Essential Criteria:

  • Domain Authority (DA) 30+: Higher is better, but relevance trumps raw DA
  • Real organic traffic: Use tools like Ahrefs or SEMrush to verify traffic
  • US-based audience: Ensures your content reaches the right market
  • Do-follow backlinks: Links that pass SEO authority
  • Editorial standards: Sites that review and edit content properly
  • Technology niche relevance: AI, SaaS, cybersecurity, fintech, etc.
  • Transparent reporting: Live URLs and performance metrics

Are Bought Guest Posts Safe for SEO?

Yes, bought guest posts are safe for SEO when done correctly. Here's how to stay safe:

  • Buy from legitimate sites: Real technology websites with editorial standards
  • Avoid PBNs: Private Blog Networks violate Google's guidelines
  • Focus on quality: One high-quality post is better than 10 low-quality ones
  • Ensure content value: Posts should provide real value to readers
  • Natural anchor text: Avoid over-optimized exact-match keywords
  • Diversify: Mix guest posts with other link building strategies

For more details on do-follow backlinks specifically for the AI niche, check out our guide on do-follow backlinks AI niche.

Red Flags: Guest Post Services to Avoid

  • Unusually cheap: Quality costs money. $50-100 per post is a red flag
  • No transparency: They won't show you where posts will be published
  • Guaranteed rankings: No legitimate service can guarantee #1 rankings
  • Private Blog Networks (PBNs): These violate Google's guidelines
  • Automated content: Low-quality, spun, or AI-generated content without editing
  • No reporting: You should get detailed reports showing every published post

How to Maximize ROI When Buying Guest Posts

Getting the most from your guest post investment requires strategy:

1. Focus on Relevance Over DA

A DA 40 site in your exact niche is more valuable than a DA 70 site in an unrelated industry.

2. Prioritize Do-Follow Links

Ensure your posts include do-follow backlinks that pass SEO authority.

3. Invest in Quality Content

High-quality, valuable content performs better and builds long-term authority.

4. Diversify Anchor Text

Use a mix of branded, generic, and keyword-rich anchor texts naturally.

5. Track Results

Monitor traffic, rankings, and conversions from each guest post.

Guest Posting on Tech Websites: Best Practices

When buying guest posts on technology websites, follow these best practices:

  • Research the site: Ensure it has real traffic and engagement
  • Review guidelines: Follow each site's editorial standards
  • Provide value: Create content that helps readers, not just promotes you
  • Include CTAs: Add subtle calls-to-action that drive conversions
  • Promote published posts: Share on social media and email newsletters
  • Build relationships: Develop long-term partnerships with editors

Measuring Success: What Metrics Matter?

✅ Key Performance Indicators: Domain Authority growth, organic traffic increases, keyword ranking improvements, referral traffic from guest posts, backlink quality metrics, brand mention increases, and most importantly—qualified leads and sales attributed to guest posting efforts.

Timeline: When Will You See Results?

Buying guest posts delivers results faster than organic outreach, but patience is still required:

  • Week 1-2: Content creation and submission
  • Week 2-4: Publication and indexing
  • Month 1-2: Initial backlink authority transfer
  • Month 2-3: Noticeable ranking improvements
  • Month 3-6: Significant traffic increases
  • Month 6-12: Compounding authority growth

Questions to Ask Before Buying Guest Posts

  1. "Can you show me examples of technology sites where you place posts?"
  2. "What's your average domain authority for published posts?"
  3. "Do you provide do-follow or no-follow links?"
  4. "How do you ensure content quality and relevance?"
  5. "What's your process for topic approval and content creation?"
  6. "Do you provide monthly reporting with live URLs?"
  7. "What's your revision policy if content gets rejected?"
