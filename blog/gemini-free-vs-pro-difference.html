AI Tools Comparisons

Gemini's free plan changed twice in five months, and the paid plans changed around it. That's why so many "Gemini Free vs Pro" articles contradict each other: some describe the plan as it was in spring, others as it is now. The short version is that Free still does a lot, but it's more rationed than it used to be, and the real price of Google AI Pro depends on something most comparisons skip, which is what you already pay Google for storage. Here's how the plans stack up as of October 21, 2026, where the sources I read disagree, and a way to work out your own answer.

What's the difference between Gemini Free and Google AI Pro?

Gemini Free costs $0 and now picks the model for you automatically, with usage limits that refresh every five hours up to a weekly cap. Google AI Pro costs $19.99 a month and adds roughly four times the limits, a 1 million token context window, expanded Deep Research, Veo video credits and 5 TB of storage.

The three differences that actually matter

1. Limits: how often you hit the wall

On Free, you get a baseline that refreshes every five hours, plus a weekly ceiling. Light users never notice. Someone who runs Deep Research or long documents through it does, usually in the middle of something. Pro's roughly fourfold allowance is the clearest reason to pay, because it removes friction rather than adding a feature.

2. Context: how much it can read at once

Tokens are the unit models read in, and a rough rule is that one token is about three quarters of a word. By that arithmetic, 32K tokens is around 24,000 words, enough for a long article or a chapter. A 1 million token window is around 750,000 words, more than most novels. If you never paste anything bigger than an email, the larger window is wasted on you. If you work with contracts, research papers or codebases, it's the difference between asking a question and splitting a document into pieces.

3. Storage: the part nobody prices honestly

Pro bundles Google storage, and that changes the maths. If you already pay for a larger Google One plan, the AI upgrade doesn't cost $19.99. It costs the difference, because the storage you were paying for comes with it. If you only use the free 15 GB, the whole price is for the AI features. Use the calculator below with your own numbers.

⚠️ Common Mistake: Comparing $19.99 with $0 and stopping there. If you're already paying for storage, or about to, the honest comparison is Pro against Free plus a storage plan.

What Free still does well

It isn't a demo. Free still covers everyday questions, writing help, image generation, voice conversations with Gemini Live, Canvas for drafting and Gems for saved custom assistants, according to the guides I read, with limited Deep Research. For a student, a casual user or someone drafting emails, that's plenty. The honest weakness is the Auto model: you can't pick the strongest one for a hard problem, and on busy days the system may route you to the lighter one.

The student question

Student pricing is the least reliable part of any Gemini guide. Two October guides say eligible US college students can claim a free year of AI Pro. An earlier roundup I used for a different article said a free student year had closed in March. Both can't be fully right at once, so look at Google's own student page for your country before you count on it. If it's available to you, it's the best deal on this page, and the free AI tools for students guide covers what else to put around it.

The Gemini app is not the Gemini API

Everything above is about the consumer app and Google's subscription plans. Developers who call Gemini from code are on a separate free tier with different limits, covered in the Gemini API free tier guide. Paying for AI Pro doesn't raise your API limits, and the API free tier doesn't give you the app's features. It's one of the most common mix-ups in comparison articles.

Before you pay anyone, compare the free alternatives

The free field is competitive. OpenAI recently removed the text cap on ChatGPT Free, which the ChatGPT Free vs Plus comparison covers, and Claude's free plan works on a rolling window explained in the Claude free plan limits guide. Many people use two free assistants and skip subscriptions entirely.
