A Brisbane plumbing & gas company was stuck at position 15 for "emergency plumber brisbane" — the single highest-value keyword in their market. They had a decent website but no strategy. Local SEO is the highest-ROI marketing channel available to Australian businesses, and it compounds.

Australian search has its own rules: google.com.au favours .com.au domains, suburb-level local intent converts 3-5x higher than national queries, and AI Overviews now intercept a large share of informational searches. This guide gives you the 12 proven SEO tips we've applied across Australian client campaigns — with real data, AUD pricing, and a 90-day roadmap.

🇦 Australian Reality 2026: Google holds 90%+ of Australian search. A .com.au domain gets a trust and relevance boost on google.com.au. The Australian Search Landscape in 2026

  • google.com.au dominance: 90%+ of Australian searches; Australian users expect local results, AUD prices, and .com.au trust signals
  • .com.au advantage: requires an ABN to register, signalling a legitimate Australian business — a measurable ranking and CTR boost
  • Spelling variants: "optimise", "organise", "colour", "specialise" — Australians search with Australian English
  • Local intent: suburb and city modifiers ("plumber fortitude valley", "dentist south yarra") convert 3-5x higher
  • AI Overviews: live in Australia on informational queries — optimise to be cited
  • Seasonality: EOFY (June), Black Friday AU, Boxing Day, and Christmas drive predictable search spikes

The 12 Proven SEO Tips for Australian Websites

Tip 1: Use a .com.au Domain

TIP 1

.com.au Trust Signal ~$20/yr

Why #1: A .com.au domain requires an ABN, signalling a legitimate Australian business. Google.com.au gives .com.au sites a relevance boost for Australian queries, and Australian searchers click .com.au results at higher rates. If you're on .com and serve Australians primarily, register the .com.au and 301-redirect or migrate.

Tip 2: Optimise Google Business Profile + Local Citations

TIP 2

Local SEO Free

Why high-converting: Claim and fully optimise your Google Business Profile: accurate NAP, correct categories, weekly posts, photos, and review responses. Build consistent citations on True Local, Yellow Pages, Hipages, Airtasker, and industry directories. Local pack queries convert 3-5x higher for Australian service businesses.

Tip 3: Australian Keyword Research

TIP 3

AU Keyword Research Free + Paid

Why essential: Set Semrush, Ahrefs, or Google Keyword Planner to Australia. Use Australian spelling and suburb-level modifiers. A KD 25 Australian term is often as winnable as a KD 15 US term — Australian SERPs are less competitive than US equivalents.

Tip 4: On-Page SEO with Australian Spelling

On-Page SEO Free

Why essential: Title tags 50-60 chars with keyword first, meta descriptions 140-155 chars with CTA, one H1 per page, question H2s, clean hyphenated URLs. Use Australian English consistently — never mix spellings on one page.

Tip 5: Technical SEO + Core Web Vitals (AU Hosting)

Technical SEO Free

Why table stakes: Host on Australian or Asia-Pacific servers — a site hosted in the US adds 150-250ms latency for Australian visitors, killing LCP. Target LCP under 2.5s, INP under 200ms, CLS under 0.1. Australian NBN speeds vary widely; test with 4G throttling.

Tip 6: Schema Markup (LocalBusiness First)

Schema Markup Free

Why rich results: Add LocalBusiness schema with Australian address, ABN, and hours; plus Article, FAQ, HowTo, and BreadcrumbList schema. Validate with Google Rich Results Test. Schema powers rich snippets and AI Overview citations on google.com.au.

Tip 7: AI Overview + AEO Optimisation

AEO Optimisation Citations

Why new traffic: Structure content with question H2s + 40-60 word direct answers, tables, and FAQ schema. AI Overviews cite structured, authoritative Australian sources — being cited drives brand searches and referral clicks. See our guide on SEO for AI websites 2026.

Tip 8: Content + Topic Clusters with AU Seasonality

Topic Clusters Strategy

Why authority: One pillar page per core topic with 8-12 interlinked cluster articles. Plan content around Australian seasonality: EOFY sales (June), Black Friday AU (November), Boxing Day, Christmas, and back-to-school (January/February). Publish 6-8 weeks before each peak.

Tip 9: Australian Link Building

TIP 9

.com.au Backlinks Earned

Why compounding: Earn .com.au, .edu.au, and .gov.au links via digital PR (original Australian data studies pitched to local news), industry directories, sponsorships, and guest posts on Australian blogs. Australian domains pass stronger relevance signals on google.com.au.

Tip 10: E-E-A-T for Australian Audiences

E-E-A-T Signals Trust

Why trust wins: Author bios with credentials, ABN and contact details, Australian case studies, citations to .gov.au and .edu.au sources, and clear privacy policies compliant with the Privacy Act 1988 and Australian Consumer Law. Google rewards demonstrable Australian expertise.

Tip 11: Mobile-First (AU Mobile Usage)

Mobile-First Free

Why essential: Over 60% of Australian searches happen on mobile, often on variable NBN/mobile speeds. Responsive design, 48x48px tap targets, 16px+ fonts, and no horizontal scrolling. Google ranks your mobile version first.

Tip 12: Measure with GSC Australia Filter

GSC AU Loop Free

Why iteration wins: Filter Google Search Console to Australia to see real Australian queries, positions, and CTR. Target "striking distance" keywords (positions 5-15) for quick wins. Refresh winners every 90 days and expand winning clusters.

Australian SEO Mistakes to Avoid

⚠️ Warning: These five mistakes keep most Australian websites stuck. Avoid them all.

Mistake 1: Targeting US/UK spelling on an AU site

The Fix: Use Australian spellings (optimise, organise, colour) as primary. Australians search with Australian English; matching it signals local relevance on google.com.au.

Mistake 2: Ignoring local SEO and Google Business Profile

The Fix: For service businesses, local pack is the highest-converting real estate in Australian search. Claim your GBP, build citations on True Local and Yellow Pages, and create suburb-level landing pages.

Mistake 3: Hosting overseas with slow AU load times

The Fix: Host on Australian or Asia-Pacific servers and serve images via CDN with Australian edge nodes. Latency kills LCP and mobile rankings.

Mistake 4: Ignoring AI Overviews on google.com.au

The Fix: Optimise to be cited (question H2s, direct answers, schema) or prioritise transactional and local queries where clicks still happen.

Mistake 5: Buying "guaranteed rankings" packages

The Fix: Guarantees violate Google guidelines and usually mean PBN links that risk penalties. Compare legitimate SEO packages for Australian small business and ask for transparent reporting instead.
