What is a Link Insertion (Niche Edit)?

A link insertion, commonly known as a "niche edit," involves placing your backlink into an existing blog post that is already published, indexed by Google, and generating organic traffic. Unlike guest posting, where you have to write a brand new article from scratch and wait months for it to gain traction, a niche edit gives you immediate access to the existing "link juice" and authority of the page.
