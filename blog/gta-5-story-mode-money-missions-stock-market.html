Gaming Money Missions

The fastest way to get rich in GTA 5 story mode is to finish the story, take the payout from The Big Score, then invest that money in the stock market while Lester's assassination missions move prices. Guides report roughly $40 million per character from the final heist, which is what makes the stock plays so profitable. This guide gives the order to do things in and a live 3D simulator to practise the timing.

⚠️ Order matters: several guides advise not starting Lester's assassination missions until after The Big Score, because the more cash you have in the bank, the bigger the stock return. Cheats will not help here, since there is no money cheat (see GTA 5 cheat codes).

Step-by-step story mode money plan

  1. Finish the main story. Complete The Big Score. Guides report around $40 million for each of the three characters, depending on your crew and approach.
  2. Unlock Lester's missions. Lester sends Franklin five stock-market assassination missions. Each hit moves stock prices.
  3. Invest before the hit. Put money into the stock that will rise. One guide names Betta Pharmaceuticals (BET) on the LCN exchange for the first hit.
  4. Complete the mission. The target's death changes the market.
  5. Wait for the peak. Guides say prices peak after roughly 24 to 48 in-game hours, and sleeping fast-forwards time.
  6. Sell and repeat. Do it with all three characters, then reinvest.

How much can you make?

One popular guide claims up to $2 billion by playing the assassination stock strategy with all three characters. Treat that as a best-case figure, because results depend on how much you invest, when you sell and which stocks you pick. The key idea is simple: the more you invest, the more you earn, so do not spend your Big Score money before the stock plays.

Story mode vs online money

Story mode is far easier to make rich in than GTA Online, because you can fast-forward the stock market and keep every dollar. If you want multiplayer income, use the best places and missions to make money in GTA Online. If you simply want fun powers in story mode, read GTA 5 cheat codes or try the older GTA San Andreas cheats.

Story mode money mistakes

  • Starting hits too early. You miss out on the bigger returns from a fat bank balance.
  • Selling too soon. Prices climb after the hit, so wait for the peak.
  • Forgetting the other characters. Repeat the plan with Michael and Trevor.
  • Using cheats and expecting cash. There is no money cheat in GTA 5.
