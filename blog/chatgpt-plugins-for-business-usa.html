Building Custom GPTs with Actions for US Workflows

The GPT Store covers common US business tools — but every US company has US workflows that no public GPT addresses. That's where custom GPTs with Actions come in.

How Actions Work

Actions let custom GPTs call external US APIs via natural language. The process:

  1. Define an OpenAPI schema describing your US API endpoints — what US data they accept and return
  2. Configure authentication — OAuth 2.0 for US user-scoped access (each US user authenticates), or API keys for US system-scoped access
  3. Test with US sample data — verify ChatGPT calls the US API correctly
  4. Deploy to US users — US employees interact via natural language; ChatGPT handles the US API calls

Example: US Inventory Management GPT

The Chicago company built a custom "US Inventory Manager" GPT with Actions connected to their US PostgreSQL database. US warehouse staff can now ask:

  • "What's the US stock level for SKU-5678?" → ChatGPT queries US database

  • "Show me US slow-moving inventory" → ChatGPT analyses US sales velocity
  • "Update US stock for SKU-1234 to 200 US units" → ChatGPT writes to US database

Previously, these US tasks required the US warehouse management system UI. Now they're conversational. US warehouse staff save 90 minutes/day.

Model Context Protocol (MCP): The 2026 US Enterprise Standard

MCP is the biggest US integration advancement since the original ChatGPT Plugins. Created by Anthropic in late 2024 and adopted by OpenAI in 2025, MCP provides bidirectional, persistent, authenticated connections between ChatGPT and US enterprise systems.

Why MCP Matters for US Businesses

  • Bidirectional: Unlike Actions (which call US APIs), MCP servers can push US data to ChatGPT and receive US queries back
  • Persistent: US MCP connections stay active across US conversations — US context is maintained
  • Authenticated: US MCP supports US OAuth, US API keys, US JWT tokens, and US SAML
  • Open standard: US MCP works across US AI providers (Anthropic, OpenAI, US Google) — no US vendor lock-in
  • Context-aware: US MCP servers understand US conversation context and US user permissions

US Compliance: SOC 2, CCPA, and US Data Security

Every US ChatGPT integration — GPT Store GPTs, custom GPTs with Actions, and US MCP servers — must satisfy US compliance requirements. Here's the US framework:

  • Verify every US integration provider has a current US SOC 2 Type II report

  • US custom Actions: your US API must be SOC 2 compliant if it handles US customer data
  • US MCP servers: typically self-hosted, so US SOC 2 responsibility is yours

CCPA / CPRA (California)

  • Applies to US businesses processing US data of 100K+ California US residents
  • US GPT Store GPTs: review each GPT's US privacy policy for CCPA compliance
  • US custom GPTs: on Team and Enterprise, US company data is not used for US model training
  • US MCP servers: US data flows through your US infrastructure — you control CCPA compliance

US Security Best Practices for ChatGPT Integrations

  • Encrypt US data in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (US AES-256)
  • Monitor US MCP server logs for US anomalous access patterns
